## Full Script (with timestamps)

**[0:00 - 0:08] INTRO - Logo Reveal**
> This is CROW. Unified customer intelligence, built by B3.

**[0:08 - 0:18] PROBLEM - Data Silos**
> Your customer data is trapped. Web analytics in one place. Social media in another. Physical retail somewhere else. Three worlds that never speak to each other.

**[0:18 - 0:28] SOLUTION - Unified Hub**
> CROW changes that. One platform that pulls every customer signal -- web, social, and in-store -- into a single source of truth.

> It starts with a lightweight web SDK that captures every click, scroll, and conversion. Then, AI-powered social intelligence monitors what your customers are really saying. And finally, CCTV analytics turns your existing cameras into real-time foot traffic sensors.

> It all comes together in one dashboard. Real-time metrics, trend analysis, and AI insights you can act on immediately.

**[0:54 - 1:00] CTA - Closing**
> See every signal. Understand every customer. Get started at crow.b3.dev.

## Production Notes

- **Total word count:** ~132 words
- **Pacing:** Natural conversational pace at ~2.2 words/second
- **Tone:** Confident, clean, modern. Not hype-y. Think Stripe or Linear product video.
- **Voice direction:** Male or female, mid-range pitch, measured delivery. Slight pauses between sentences for emphasis. The CTA segment should land with conviction -- slower, more deliberate.
- **Pronunciation guide:**
  - CROW: rhymes with "go" (one syllable)
  - B3: "Bee Three"
  - SDK: "S-D-K" (spell it out)
  - CCTV: "C-C-T-V" (spell it out)
  - crow.b3.dev: "crow dot bee-three dot dev"
- **Segment 4 (features)** is the longest at 18 seconds. If pacing feels rushed, the three sub-sections map to the visual phase transitions:
  - Web SDK portion: 0:28 - 0:34 (6s)
  - Social Intelligence portion: 0:34 - 0:40 (6s)
  - CCTV Analytics portion: 0:40 - 0:46 (6s)
